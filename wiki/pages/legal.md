This is an independent hobby project for interoperability and preservation. It is
**not affiliated with Audi, Aisin AW, Grundig, Alpine, Fujitsu, NEC, Renesas or
anyone else**. Product names and part numbers are used only to identify the hardware
being described.

## No firmware here

The RNS-E's firmware is copyrighted by its owners. This site and the emulator's
source contain **no firmware, no boot ROM image, no map data, and nothing extracted
from them**. To run the emulator you need an image of a firmware update CD that you
own.

The browser version at [/emulator/](/emulator/) reads the disc image you choose
**on your own computer**, inside the page. It is never uploaded, and this site
keeps no copy of it or of anything the emulator computes from it.

## Custom discs and cards

A disc made with `rnse custom` or `rnse firmware` contains most of the update disc
it was made from. It is for use with your own unit; do not distribute it. The same
goes for images of a unit's battery-backed memory, which hold its owner's
destinations and car details, and for map data made for a particular unit.

## Your unit

Running custom software on a real RNS-E is at your own risk. Read
[Custom firmware](/custom-firmware/), in particular "The way back" and "The rules",
before putting a disc in a car. The software here has run on one bench unit, which
says nothing certain about yours.

The photographs on this site were taken by the project's author of units in their

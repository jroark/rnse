RNS-E emulator

This runs the real software of the RNS-E navigation unit on an emulated unit, inside this page. The software is not here: it comes from an update disc, and you have to bring an image of one (an .iso file of the CD).

Your files stay on your computer. They are read by this page and are not sent anywhere; the site only serves the emulator itself.

An ISO image of a software update CD, or its LOADING.KWI file.
An ISO image of a navigation DVD, or the image of an SD card prepared for the unit. Read as needed, not loaded.
